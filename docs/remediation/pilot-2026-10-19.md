# Small GPU pilot — October 19, 2026

The pilot completed its operational sequence: CPU preparation, two L40S SFT updates, six H100 GRPO updates, 32 L40S evaluation questions and an independent-container persistence check. **The research gate failed:** the final-answer scorer rejects valid prose answers. Its use in GRPO also makes reward validity an outstanding requirement. Do not launch the full study from these results.

All three pilot apps are stopped. No full study or hardware comparison was launched. The reported workspace metered-cost increase is approximately **$0.59**, within the approved $10 pilot cap. The billing snapshot shows zero billed cost after credits; provider accounting can lag. This is an observed workspace increment, not an independently attributed final invoice.

## Measured GPU work

| Stage | GPU | Compute time | Work | Sampled peak device memory | Mean sampled utilization across function |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| SFT | L40S | 104.6 s training | 2 updates; 521,338 tokens | 27.7 GiB | 70.5% |
| GRPO | H100 80GB | 110.0 s training | 6 updates; 48 rollouts; 6 unique prompts | 36.7 GiB | 38.2% |
| Evaluation | L40S | 133.1 s generation | 32 questions; 28,094 output tokens | 44.4 GiB | 69.5% |

SFT achieved approximately 4,982 processed training tokens/s during its training interval; evaluation achieved approximately 211 output tokens/s for this batch and length distribution. Neither figure establishes throughput on the full study. Utilization averages include loading, setup and export, and are not training-only utilization or achieved FLOP utilization.

GRPO setup took 49.8 seconds; SFT setup took 3.3 seconds. Complete model exports took 9.8 seconds for SFT and 12.3 seconds for GRPO. End-to-end remote-call times were longer: 180.0 seconds for SFT, 211.4 seconds for successful GRPO and 162.7 seconds for evaluation. The profiles therefore support separating fixed costs from repeated compute in future quotes.

Evaluation's Torch allocator peak was **11.1 GiB allocated versus 43.9 GiB reserved**. The much larger device-memory sample includes cached allocator memory. Do not interpret the device peak as 44.4 GiB of live tensors or assume that the batch nearly exhausts irreclaimable memory. Cache/allocation behavior and batch-size alternatives warrant a controlled benchmark. Primary batch 32 completed; long-context and sampled protocols remain unvalidated.

H100 remains an unproven cost choice. Its lower whole-function utilization alone does not show that L40S would be cheaper: decode bandwidth, setup and completion lengths differ. Compare equivalent successful work on both GPUs before choosing main-study hardware.

## Failures found and recovery

1. The first app stopped during image construction, before dispatching pilot tasks. Offline environment settings were attached after local source mounts, which Modal rejects. `image_for()` now sets the environment before adding local files. The first app's state and billing were reconciled before retrying.
2. SFT then completed, but the first GRPO attempt failed before its first update with a CUDA invalid-argument error while loading vLLM weights. The pinned TRL code calls weight synchronization before the wake-up in generation, after initializing the engine asleep. This ordering and the failure site support the diagnosis of copying into sleeping weight buffers. See the [pinned generation implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/trl/blob/v0.28.0/trl/generation/vllm_generation.py). Sleep mode is disabled for the pinned stack. The subsequent six-update run succeeded with the models resident.
3. Recovery reused the completed CPU preparation and immutable SFT export. It did not repeat SFT. The stopped failed app, original ledger, corrected-source recovery plan, reused-node provenance and reduced recovery allowance are recorded in [`pilot-grpo-reconciliation.json`](../../results/recovery/pilot-grpo-reconciliation.json). The recovery runner's cap was $6.45252 after conservatively holding the original $2.54748 reservation and an additional $1 allowance within the original $10 approval.

The original ledger is `outputs/studies/855e33aa554617b8489e4e56/ledger.json`; the completed recovery ledger is `outputs/studies/e0d016d28610bfdc294d97b0/ledger.json`. ## Research blockers

### Numeric prose extraction

The recorded v2 result is 3/32 correct. There are 21 `unparseable` responses and eight `unfinished_thinking` responses. The raw text exposes clear false negatives among completed prose answers, for example:

- Question 0: the model concludes that Janet makes **$18 per day**, matching the ground truth, but extraction fails.
- Question 1: the model explicitly gives **3 bolts**, matching the ground truth, but extraction fails.
- Question 4: the model explicitly gives **20 cups** for the final meal, matching the ground truth, but extraction fails.

The extractor's final marker/whole-expression rules are too restrictive for natural-language answers. These examples do not imply that every unparseable answer is correct. No replacement accuracy was chosen after seeing the pilot. Preserve the v2 records, develop a versioned correction against broader independent fixtures, retain rejection of unfinished thinking/ambiguous expressions, and rescore retained evaluation responses on CPU. Then repeat a bounded GRPO pilot because its rewards used the same faulty extractor.

Three of six GRPO updates had zero within-group reward variance and zero gradient. The small sample and scorer defects prevent attributing that entirely to model ability or dataset difficulty. The vendor's aggregate `reward` log sums unweighted reward components even when the format component's configured weight is zero; interpret correctness and weighted-objective diagnostics separately.

### Token-accounting erratum

The original GRPO metadata reports `processed_train_tokens: 0` from a generic trainer counter. The saved trainer state instead records **54,492 total prompt-plus-completion tokens**, and the reward callback independently records **50,596 completion tokens**. The original metadata is preserved; the corrected interpretation is recorded in `pilot-outcome.json`. Future reporting should read GRPO's `trainer.state.num_input_tokens_seen` instead of the generic SFT counter.
